# Learning goals

## Goals

One or two sentences: what the learner wants to be able to do with Claude Code.

## Time horizon

When they want to get there, for example this month or this quarter.

## Preferred depth

Overview, working knowledge or deep expertise; short lessons or long ones.

## Topics of interest

Topics to prioritise, such as hooks, skills or subagents.

## Topics to avoid

Topics to skip or keep quiet about.
